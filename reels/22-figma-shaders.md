# Free Figma shaders

A reel by [@design4me__](https://www.instagram.com/design4me__/) (posted 2026-09-23). Source: [original reel](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DdoF2QPICyz/). Caption: "free shaders alert", a collection of "the coolest figma shaders i could find", the visual effects that make a design feel more interesting. The roughly 10-second video was recovered from the public media data and its frames inspected. On-screen text: "figma shaders you can actually steal", then a label per effect, and at the end "drop comment 'shade' to get the list". Seven effects appear, in this order.

## Items

### 126. Lens distortion

Figma shader effect that creates realistic lens distortion and chromatic aberration with an adjustable center point (fisheye-style bulge plus colour fringing). Shown first in the reel on a close-up portrait.

- [Figma Community page](https://www.figma.com/community/shader/1650660511777647690/lens-distortion)

### 127. Moving blobs

Animated, looping low-frequency noise mapped to a choice of refraction, displacement, bulge/pinch, hue shift or invert. The reel shows it warping a floral image into drifting blobs. Listed under the Figma Community profile.

- [Figma Community page](https://www.figma.com/community/shader/1676361487169314995/moving-blobs)

### 128. Slice shift

Slices an image into angled bands and shifts them apart, with adjustable angle, spread and randomness. The reel shows it splitting a face into offset strips.

- [Figma Community page](https://www.figma.com/community/shader/1650668252069936227/slice-shift)

### 129. Pixel stretch

Creates stretch and motion-inspired distortion effects, edited visually with on-canvas controls. The reel drags a circular control over an illustration to smear it. Page says it was built with the Figma agent.

- [Figma Community page](https://www.figma.com/community/shader/1650660519331906639/pixel-stretch)

### 130. Halftone

Simulates vintage print and comic-book halftones, with controls for colour separation, softness, scale and placement. The reel applies it to an illustrated landscape.

- [Figma Community page](https://www.figma.com/community/shader/1650660527370397779/halftone)

### 131. Warp

Creates organic distortions like ripples, swirls, twists and bulges (description from Figma's help centre). The reel shows it bending a red and black graphic.

- [Figma Community page](https://www.figma.com/community/shader/1650660506105824327/warp)

### 132. Pixelate

Pixelates and scatters an image into tiled shapes, with adjustable size, colour reduction and dissolve. The reel ends on it breaking a photo into blocks. Page says it was built with the Figma agent.

- [Figma Community page](https://www.figma.com/community/shader/1650660504217386054/pixelate)
