# Bloomberg covers for design inspiration

Oren John (brand and creative director; caption tag #creativedirector) holds up print Bloomberg Businessweek issues as cover-design inspiration, posted 2026-10-08. The frame shows him presenting several covers, including "A Very Long Bull" (September 17, 2018 issue) and the "Let's Get It On" United-Continental cover (February 2012). Businessweek's cover direction is a decades-long design touchstone: Richard Turley's 2010-2014 creative-director era made the weekly cover reveal an event in itself, and the magazine still commissions illustrated and concept covers today. The reel names no resources, so the items below gather the verified places to browse the covers, the design coverage explaining the famous era, and the two covers visible in the frame. Links checked 2026-10-11.

- Portfolio: https://awpoop.com/Bloomberg-Businessweek-Covers

## 85. Internet Archive: Business Week scans

Kind: archive-item
Collection: archives
Creator: Internet Archive

The pub_business-week collection: 1,890 scanned Business Week issues from 1932 onward (SIM microfilm), free to borrow with an Internet Archive account. The full-run vintage counterpart to Bloomberg's official archive, which covers recent years only.

- Collection: https://archive.org/details/pub_business-week

## 86. EBSCO Bloomberg Businessweek Magazine Archive

Kind: archive-item
Collection: archives
Creator: EBSCO

Billed as the only complete digital backfile: 4,650 issues, 1929-2010, cover-to-cover as originally published. Library/institutional access rather than open web - check a university or public library subscription.

- Details: https://about.ebsco.com/products/magazine-archives/bloomberg-businessweek-magazine-archive

## 87. magCulture: Bloomberg Businessweek redesigned

Kind: website
Collection: design-coverage
Creator: Jeremy Leslie (magCulture, 2017)

Explains why the 2009-2013 Tyrangiel-Turley Businessweek became an editorial-design touchstone ("a classic blend of restraint and mania", the weekly cover reveal as its own event) and reviews the quieter 2017 redesign under creative director Robert Vargas.

- Article: https://magculture.com/blogs/journal/bloomberg-businessweek-redesigned

## 88. designboom: Richard Turley interview

Kind: website
Collection: design-coverage
Creator: designboom (2013)

Turley on the 2010 reinvention in his own words: the redrawn Helvetica, the strict Guardian-learned grid he wanted his 15-person art department to "fuck up", the Wednesday 7PM ship cycle, and his favorite special issues (2010 year in review, 2011 Steve Jobs, 2012 election).

- Interview: https://www.designboom.com/design/richard-turley-bloomberg-businessweek-interview/

## 89. Bloomberg: On the Cover Trail with Richard Turley

Kind: website
Collection: design-coverage
Creator: Bloomberg LP (2014)

Bloomberg's own press piece on the cover team (Turley, assistant CD Tracy Ma, design director Cynthia Hoffman, photo director David Carthas, art director Robert Vargas) and the weekly cover process, citing the mating-airplanes and suicidal-Twitter-bird covers.

- Article: https://www.bloomberg.com/company/press/cover-trail-richard-turley/

## 90. D&AD: Bloomberg Businessweek Cover Series

Kind: website
Collection: design-coverage
Creator: D&AD

2016 Wood Pencil winner in Magazine & Newspaper Design. The awards entry states the case for the covers: they "interpret complex news into succinct visual solutions" and upend what a business magazine cover should be.

- Entry: https://www.dandad.org/work/d-ad-awards-archive/bloomberg-businessweek-cover-series
