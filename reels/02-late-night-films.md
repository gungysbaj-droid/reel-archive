# Three films to watch alone late at night

Fallen Angels, Le Samouraï, Blow Out. Years, directors, actors, plots checked against Criterion. Links are reference pages, not streaming availability in Japan.

## 25. Fallen Angels (1995)

Kind: film
Collection: films
Creator: Wong Kar-wai

Lonely people drift through Hong Kong, crossing paths and trying to connect. Max highlights the dreamlike neon, distorted wide-angle lenses and smeared motion. His pick for ghostly, restless city atmosphere.

- Criterion: https://www.criterion.com/films/28842-fallen-angels

## 26. Le Samouraï (1967)

Kind: film
Collection: films
Creator: Jean-Pierre Melville

Alain Delon plays Jef Costello, a contract killer caught between the police and his employer. Max focuses on its spare story, precise compositions and cool style. His pick for a solitary, doomed-hitman mood.

Image: ../assets/20-film-3.jpg

- Criterion: https://www.criterion.com/films/184-le-samourai

## 27. Blow Out (1981)

Kind: film
Collection: films
Creator: Brian De Palma

John Travolta plays a sound-effects technician who believes he has recorded a political assassination in Philadelphia. Max describes feeling paranoid throughout, drawn into a late-night investigation. He praises the ending without revealing it.

Image: ../assets/19-film-2.jpg

- Criterion: https://www.criterion.com/films/27561-blow-out
