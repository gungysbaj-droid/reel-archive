# Books to develop your taste as a creative

A carousel post (not a reel) by [@processthepodcast](https://www.instagram.com/processthepodcast/) (Process The Podcast), posted 2026-09-17. Source: [original post](https://www.instagram.com/p/DdYbn9Ciajo/). Caption: "How to train your eye and build a sharper creative instinct! From Rick Rubin to Josef Albers, here are the books gracing our bedside table that will completely change the way you look at colour, form, and everyday details." The first slide, titled "Books recommendations to develop: Your Taste as a Creative", shows six numbered covers. Slide 2 spotlights two of them. Only these two slides were readable from the signed-out embed; any later slides were not seen.

## Items

### 138. The Creative Act: A Way of Being

Rick Rubin. Slide 1 item 01. Slide 2: "The book that builds your creative instinct."

- [Penguin Random House](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/717356/the-creative-act-by-rick-rubin/)

### 139. On Beauty and Being Just

Elaine Scarry. Slide 1 item 02. Slide 2: "The book that sharpens your sense of beauty." The slide spells the author "Ellaine"; the cover and publisher use Elaine Scarry.

- [Princeton University Press](https://press.princeton.edu/books/paperback/9780691089591/on-beauty-and-being-just)

### 140. The Art of Looking Sideways

Alan Fletcher, Phaidon. Slide 1 item 03. The slide shows no title; the archive identified it from the visible cover text ("the art of looking sideways") and the Phaidon logo.

- [Internet Archive](https://archive.org/details/artoflookingside0000flet) (reference link, not a rights claim)
- [Designers & Books](https://www.designersandbooks.com/book/art-looking-sideways)

### 141. Interaction of Color

Josef Albers. Slide 1 item 04. The cover shown is the 50th Anniversary Edition (Yale University Press).

- [Yale University Press](https://yalebooks.yale.edu/book/9780300179354/interaction-of-color/)

### 142. Dieter Rams: As Little Design as Possible

Sophie Lovell, Phaidon. Slide 1 item 05. The author credit comes from the publisher listing, not the slide.

- [Phaidon](https://www.phaidon.com/en-us/products/dieter-rams-as-little-design-as-possible)

### 143. Design as Art

Bruno Munari, Penguin Modern Classics. Slide 1 item 06.

- [Penguin Random House](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/276505/design-as-art-by-bruno-munari/)
