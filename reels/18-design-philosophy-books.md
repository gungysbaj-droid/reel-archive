# Six books that shaped a designer's taste

@jasperjmutimer recommends six books that shaped his design taste. His caption argues that knowing at least one designer's philosophy is useful for a creative career. The six titles and their order were extracted from the reel's own caption. Publisher and library pages verify the books and the coverage notes below. Video frames could not be recovered from the signed-out embed, so no unobserved on-screen commentary is claimed. Links checked 2026-10-11.

## Coverage and limits

These are source-based coverage notes, not full texts or chapter-by-chapter reading notes. Copyrighted book contents are not reproduced. No cover images were captured from the reel.

## 113. Tokyo Style

Kind: book
Collection: design-philosophy-books
Creator: Kyoichi Tsuzuki

Photographic record of Tokyo homes as they are lived in: clutter, collecting, improvised storage and personal taste rather than styled interiors. Asia Art Archive describes visits to a hundred homes over two years and eight sections: Beauty in Chaos, The Fancy Fetish, Artsy Pads, The Traditional Touch, Monomaniacs, Kiddie Kingdoms, Inertial Living and Hermitages. The work was first published in 1993; the linked Apartamento edition (2024, reprinted 2025) adds a foreword by Barry Yourgrau and a new afterword by Tsuzuki. The reel does not specify an edition.

- Publisher / current edition: https://www.apartamentomagazine.com/product/tokyo-style-kyoichi-tsuzuki-book/
- Library record / contents: https://aaa.org.hk/en/collections/search/library/tokyo-style

## 114. MAKI OPUS

Kind: book
Collection: design-philosophy-books
Creator: Fumihiko Maki

Career retrospective of architect Fumihiko Maki, published by Thames & Hudson and compiled with his studio. The publisher describes fifty buildings from 1960 onward, including MIT Media Lab, the Aga Khan Museum and 4 World Trade Centre. Coverage follows his materials, building technology and work outside Japan, using archival photographs and drawings. The caption names the publisher, not an author.

- Publisher: https://www.thamesandhudson.com/products/maki-opus

## 115. Dieter Rams: The Complete Works

Kind: book
Collection: design-philosophy-books
Creator: Klaus Klemp

Phaidon's chronological catalogue of the products designed by Dieter Rams, made in collaboration with him and with his foreword. Each product has a description and technical specifications. The publisher situates the work around Rams's ten principles for good design. This is The Complete Works, not the separate title As Little Design as Possible.

- Publisher: https://www.phaidon.com/en-us/products/dieter-rams-the-complete-works

## 116. Eames

Kind: book
Collection: design-philosophy-books
Creator: Gloria Koenig

TASCHEN's Basic Art introduction to Charles and Ray Eames. It covers furniture, architecture, textile design, photography and film, from molded plywood furniture and the Case Study Houses to Powers of Ten. A compact overview of their cross-disciplinary practice, rather than only a chair catalogue.

- Publisher: https://www.taschen.com/en/books/architecture-design/49209/eames/

## 117. Brett Whiteley: Art & Life

Kind: book
Collection: design-philosophy-books
Creator: Barry Pearce, with Bryan Robertson and Wendy Whiteley

Thames & Hudson survey of Whiteley's work from the 1950s to the final years of his life, with over 180 colour plates. Barry Pearce provides the overview of his life and art; Bryan Robertson writes about the London years; Wendy Whiteley contributes a personal portrait. The publisher emphasizes landscape, the nude and fluid drawing. This is the art survey, not Ashleigh Wilson's biography Art, Life and the Other Thing.

- Publisher: https://www.thamesandhudson.com/products/brett-whiteley

## 118. Vitra: The Anatomy of a Design Company

Kind: book
Collection: design-philosophy-books
Creator: Deyan Sudjic, with contributions by Karen Stein and Iwan Baan

Phaidon history of three generations behind Vitra and its role across architecture, graphics, photography and product design. Coverage includes work with the Eameses, Gehry and Hadid, support for designers such as Jasper Morrison and the Bouroullecs, preserved design archives and the design museum. The verified edition is published by Phaidon; the caption names Sudjic without naming a publisher.

- Publisher: https://www.phaidon.com/en-us/products/vitra
