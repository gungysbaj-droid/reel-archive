# Two books by Louis-Ferdinand Céline

"Beautiful books written by disgusting authors". Reel calls it 19th century French lit, but both books are 1930s. Quote "I'm not a man of ideas, I'm a man of style" is from reel captions, unchecked.

Source reel: https://www.instagram.com/reel/DdnbeoaqKwF/

## Items

### 55. Journey to the End of the Night (1932)
- kind: book
- collection: celine (French lit)
- creator: Louis-Ferdinand Céline
- image: ../assets/cel-2.jpg
- link (Wikipedia): https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Journey_to_the_End_of_the_Night

His first novel, called one of the masterpieces of 20th century French literature in the reel. Semi-autobiographical: follows Ferdinand Bardamu through World War I, colonial Africa, the United States and the poor suburbs of Paris.

### 56. Death on the Installment Plan (1936)
- kind: book
- collection: celine (French lit)
- creator: Louis-Ferdinand Céline
- image: ../assets/cel-1.jpg
- link (Wikipedia): https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Death_on_Credit

The creator’s favorite: she loved it from the first pages. Semi-autobiographical, about his childhood growing up in the slums of Paris. Filled with hatred and disgust but also dreams and fantasies, and so visceral you can almost smell the dirtiness. Wikipedia lists the same 1936 novel as Death on Credit.
