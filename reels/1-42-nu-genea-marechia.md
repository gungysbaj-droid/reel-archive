# Day 184: 'Marechia' by Nu Genea

Source: [original reel](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DemYZwpPPgm/).

An episode of machi7k's daily series "Discovering Music From Around The World" (Day 184), posted 2026-10-16 (24.8K likes, 156 comments at save). Caption: "From Napoli to the Mediterranean: @nu_genea are bridging the diverse identities, languages, and sounds of the mediterranean into music that's impossible to dislike" (#napoli #disco #funk #mediterranean #italy). He wears a 1980s SSC Napoli (Buitoni) shirt and holds up his own vinyl copies. Sampled frames across the full 93-second video were visually inspected.

The featured track is **'Marechia'** by **Nu Genea** (with Celia Kameni), from **Bar Mediterraneo** (2022). The reel then traces the Neapolitan duo (Massimo Di Lena and Lucio Aquilina): they started in techno, made a first album built on Tony Allen's afrobeat drum patterns (The Tony Allen Experiments, 2016, under their old name Nu Guinea), then Nuova Napoli (2018, "a love letter to Napoli"), helped curate the Napoli Segreta compilations of obscure Neapolitan recordings, renamed themselves Nu Genea (Napoli's name comes from the Greek "Nea Polis"), and reached Bar Mediterraneo (2022, on their own NG Records) and the new album People of the Moon (2026). He describes Bar Mediterraneo as "an imaginary bar" where people "separated by languages [are] united by the sea and by music" - the album's own liner notes use the same line. Sounds flagged along the way include French and Tunisian Arabic vocals and an Anatolian zurna.

His end-screen list of favorite Nu Genea songs: Bar Mediterraneo, Marechia, People of the Moon, Tienate, Praja Magia, Acelera, Ddoje Facce, Disco Sole, Scialla.

## Items

#### 236. 'Marechia' - Nu Genea (with Celia Kameni)

The song featured in the reel, from Bar Mediterraneo (2022). Per the album notes, "unbridled happiness and sun ooze from the delicate vocals of Celia Kameni and create an acrobatic bridge between French and Neapolitan language."

- [Bar Mediterraneo album (Bandcamp)](https://nugenea.bandcamp.com/album/bar-mediterraneo)
- [Nu Genea on Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/nu_genea/)

#### 237. Bar Mediterraneo - Nu Genea (2022, NG Records)

The vinyl he holds up first (NG Records NG 05). Nine tracks: Bar Mediterraneo, Tienate, Gelbi (with Marzouk Mejri, sung in Tunisian dialect), Marechia (with Celia Kameni), Straniero (built on Tony Allen drum patterns), Praja Magia, Vesuvio, Rire (with Marco Castello), La Crisi (adapting a 1930 Raffaele Viviani poem). Conceived as an imaginary shared bar where travelers' languages and sounds fuse; released May 13, 2022.

- [Bar Mediterraneo (Bandcamp)](https://nugenea.bandcamp.com/album/bar-mediterraneo)

#### 238. People of the Moon - Nu Genea (2026, NG Records)

The new album he holds up at the end (NG Records NG07, released May 1, 2026). Ten tracks with guests Tom Misch (Onenon), Maria Jose Llergo (Acelera, Celavi), Fabiana Martone (Puleza), Celinatique (Shway Shway) and Gabriel Prado (Ondas Do Mar), plus Care, People of the Moon, Ma Tu Che Bbuo (the track with the Anatolian zurna) and first single Scialla (summer 2025). Sung in Neapolitan, Arabic, English, Spanish and Portuguese.

- [People of the Moon (Bandcamp)](https://nugenea.bandcamp.com/album/people-of-the-moon)

#### 239. Nuova Napoli - Nu Genea (2018)

Their second album, "a love letter to Napoli" in the reel's words. Seven tracks: Nuova Napoli, Je Vulesse, Ddoje Facce, Disco Sole, Stann Fore, 'A Voce 'E Napule, Parev' Ajere. (Ddoje Facce and Disco Sole are in his end-screen favorites.)

- [Nuova Napoli (Bandcamp)](https://nugenea.bandcamp.com/album/nuova-napoli)

#### 240. The Tony Allen Experiments - Nu Guinea (2016, Comet Records / Early Sounds)

Their first album, "built with Tony Allen" in the reel's words: Nu Guinea re-working original drum patterns from the afrobeat master into electronic jazz-psyche-funk. Third volume of Comet Records' Afrobeat Makers Series, with Early Sounds Recordings, LP out February 2016; recorded under their original name Nu Guinea.

- [The Tony Allen Experiments (Comet Records)](https://cometrec.fr/portfolio/nu-genea/)
- [The Tony Allen Experiments (Afrobeat Makers Bandcamp)](https://afrobeatmakers.bandcamp.com/album/nu-genea-the-tony-allen-experiments)

#### 241. Napoli Segreta vol.1 & vol.2 - various artists (compilations)

The crate-digger compilations of obscure 1970s-80s Neapolitan music the reel says the duo helped curate (both covers shown on screen). Vol.1 (Early Sounds Recordings) collects Donatella Viggiano, Giancarlo D'Auria, Tonica & Dominante, Oro, Antonio Sorrentino and more; Vol.2 came out on the duo's own NG Records.

- [Napoli Segreta vol.1 (Early Sounds Bandcamp)](https://earlysounds.bandcamp.com/album/napoli-segreta-vol-1)
- [Napoli Segreta Vol.2 (NG Records Bandcamp)](https://ngrecords.bandcamp.com/album/napoli-segreta-vol-2)

#### 242. Nu Genea (Massimo Di Lena & Lucio Aquilina)

The duo itself: Naples-formed producers, formerly Nu Guinea, renamed Nu Genea (the reel ties the name to Napoli's Greek origin). They run their own label, NG Records. Their sound: sun-drenched electronic funk/disco bridging Neapolitan, Mediterranean and global sounds.

- [Nu Genea (Bandcamp)](https://nugenea.bandcamp.com/)
- [NG Records label (Bandcamp)](https://ngrecords.bandcamp.com/)
- [Nu Genea (Wikipedia)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nu_Genea)
