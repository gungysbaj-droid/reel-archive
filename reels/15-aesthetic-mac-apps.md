# Three aesthetic Mac apps

Mac customization creator fensyy19 (Fensy) shares 3 aesthetic Mac apps (posted 2026-10-08): Vinyl (Spotify and Apple Music as a vinyl player), Launchy (radial app menu) and Droppy (notch animations and widgets). Download links are pinned in the comments, and a Droppy discount link is in the bio; neither was visible signed-out. The reel names all three, so the archive verifies each app's own page. Links checked 2026-10-11.

## 105. Vinyl - album art as a turntable on your desktop

Kind: website
Collection: aesthetic-mac-apps
Creator: shhivv

Native macOS app that puts a photorealistic animated turntable on the screen, showing the album art and details of whatever is playing in Spotify or Apple Music (per its site). Matches the reel's first pick. A commenter on the reel says Vinyl isn't free; the maker also sells it through Gumroad. Pricing not checked, and there is an open-source GitHub repo by the same author.

- Site: https://www.vinylformac.com/
- Source repo: https://github.com/shhivv/vinylformac
- Gumroad: https://shhiv.gumroad.com/l/vinyl

## 106. Launchy - radial app launcher and switcher

Kind: website
Collection: aesthetic-mac-apps
Creator: iSapozhnik

macOS app giving quick access to launch or switch between applications through a radial menu, which fits the reel's "radial menu" description. Caveat: several unrelated apps are called Launchy (one is a Launchpad replacement at launchy.website); this is the radial one. The reel's "Liquid Glass" claim and exact app match are not confirmed from the video.

- GitHub: https://github.com/iSapozhnik/Launchy-App-Switcher-Launcher
- Product Hunt: https://www.producthunt.com/products/launchy-app-launcher-switcher

## 107. Droppy - Dynamic Island for the Mac notch

Kind: website
Collection: aesthetic-mac-apps
Creator: Droppy

Mac app that turns the notch (or a floating pill) into a Dynamic Island-style surface: now playing, live activities, shelf and HUDs, with Liquid Glass and animation design written up in its blog. Matches the reel's third pick. The reel says a discount link is in the creator's bio; not checked.

- Site: https://getdroppy.app/
- Notch docs: https://getdroppy.app/docs/notch
- How Droppy animates: https://getdroppy.app/blog/how-droppy-animates
