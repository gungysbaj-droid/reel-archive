## Items

### 122. Eaten by the Internet

Edited by Corinne Cath, Meatspace Press. Fifteen chapters on internet infrastructure as political power. Reel: "this one gave me a more critical lens" and "the vocabulary for what's happening online".

- [Publisher](https://shop.meatspacepress.com/products/eaten-by-the-internet)
- [Meatspace Press](https://meatspacepress.com/)
- [Internet Archive](https://archive.org/details/eaten-by-the-internet) (reference link, not a rights claim)

### 123. Becoming the Product

Morgane Billuart, Set Margins'. 164-page essay on critical internet researchers (Lovink, Citarella, Quicho, Publig) and the attention economy. Reel: "it dives into the internet economy that runs on attention, aesthetics and influence".

- [Publisher](https://www.setmargins.press/books/becoming-the-product/)

### 124. Slanted Magazine #47: Digital Tools

Spring/Summer 2026 issue on the tools behind creative practice. Reel: "a fun read on how digital tools..." (spreads shown).

- [Publisher](https://www.slanted.de/slanted-magazine-47-digital-tools/)
- [Digital Tools directory](https://www.slanted.de/news/digital-tools/)

### 125. Third Place Zine

Independent print and digital zine on community in the built environment; the reel's "bonus for when you actually want to log off", on how people build communities in unexpected places. The exact issue shown was not identified; the site currently lists Issue 2: Movement Building.

- [Official site](https://thirdplacezine.com/)
- [Substack](https://thirdplacezine.substack.com/)
