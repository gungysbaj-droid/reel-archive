# Six portfolios that feel physical

Source: [original reel](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DeZLTwLoss7/).

Curated-list reel by @design4me__, posted 2026-10-12 (3,751 likes, 802 comments at save). Caption: "new curated portfolio list. This time, I picked portfolios that feel like they somehow made their way from the physical world into the digital one. So personal, so tactile, and full of little details that tell you more about the designer than their work ever could. Comment 'INSPO' and I'll send you the link!" Screen-recorded on a monitor in a red-lit room, with each site's URL typeset on screen. The ~10-second video was inspected frame by frame; all six URLs were read off the video and verified live. Comment-bait format, but every site is public and free to visit - no DM needed.

## Items

#### 261. Studio Monstro - studiomonstro.com

LA-born creative practice ("design practice"): brands, spaces, places and things, with a collage-style site full of tactile scraps (the "GOOD NIGHT" spread shown in the reel).

- [studiomonstro.com](https://studiomonstro.com/)

#### 262. Analogue - madebyanalogue.co.uk

Brand and motion studio; the reel shows its loud pink toy-camera/brutalist page ("brand and motion studio" label on screen).

- [madebyanalogue.co.uk](https://madebyanalogue.co.uk/)

#### 263. Pesselev Collective - pesselev-collective.com

Product designer Sonya Pesselev's portfolio disguised as her living room: a wall of vintage TVs, each TV a different room of the work ("Welcome to the Collective").

- [pesselev-collective.com](https://pesselev-collective.com/)

#### 264. A24 concept site - a24.raviklaassens.com

Designer Ravi Klaassens' A24 tribute/concept: a shelf of A24 films as disc artwork (Backrooms, The Invite) with review quotes - a fake-physical movie collection.

- [a24.raviklaassens.com](https://a24.raviklaassens.com/)

#### 265. Sumner Howells ($CUMNER) - scumner.world

Product designer Sumner Howells' playful portfolio behind a joke gate ("Password Hint: Type Anything Diva"), opening into a blue-sky desktop world.

- [scumner.world](https://scumner.world/)

#### 266. Lea Harmoza - harmozalea.com

"Photographer's Book as an Unintuitive Experience": Lea Harmoza's photo book as a website you explore by moving the cursor, scrolling and clicking (created by Asia Gawron). Contents include Vingt Sept and Foudre magazine work.

- [harmozalea.com](https://www.harmozalea.com/)
