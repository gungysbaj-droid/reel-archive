#### 267. WarholArts - warhol-arts.webflow.io

Dark pop-art exhibition site: giant 'WARHOL' wordmark, Warhol silkscreen portraits (the Queen, Campbell's Tomato Soup), 'Soon' wall of repeated portraits.

- [warhol-arts.webflow.io](https://warhol-arts.webflow.io/)

#### 268. amo - amo.co

Collage-style homepage: a sunflower, palm trees, beach umbrellas and cut-out objects layered over a checkerboard transparency grid, with the 'amo' wordmark. The amo social app is known for a messy fridge-magnet visual language.

- [amo.co](https://amo.co/)

#### 269. Oddly Made - oddlymade.webflow.io

'A Creative Studio Shaping Distinct Brands And Digital Experiences.' Serif headline with a handwritten 'made' script over a red video panel, plus a zig-zag stack of colourful books and a Campbell's can.

- [oddlymade.webflow.io](https://oddlymade.webflow.io/)
