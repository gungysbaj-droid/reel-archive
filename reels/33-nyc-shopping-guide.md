# My Full NYC Shopping Guide

Source: [original reel](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DechQkkRQKg/), @elliotduprey (Elliot Duprey), posted October 13, 2026. The caption reads "Everything you could ever need to shop for clothes in NYC! (Of course, there's always more gems to find!)" with tags #fashion #nyc #nycshopping #shoppingguide #secondhandfashion. The roughly 3-minute video was recovered from the public media data and its frames were inspected (one frame every 4 seconds), so store names come from the on-screen cards.

## What the video shows

He talks to camera in a flat. Title card: "My Full NYC Shopping Guide - Secondhand, New Fashion, And Everything Else!". Each store gets a card with the store, its neighborhood and its specialty, plus photos. Section 1 is Secondhand Stores (31 entries below, including chains and additional mentions). Section 2 is New Multibrand Stores. The video ends while on section 2 (Ven. Space, Atelier New York); no later section appears in this file, so the "everything else" part is not covered. Spoken content was not transcribed, only the on-screen text, so tips he says aloud are not recorded.
