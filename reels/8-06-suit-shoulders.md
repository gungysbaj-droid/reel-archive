### 54. Suit shoulder expression: roped, natural, bald
- kind: lesson
- collection: tailoring (Suit shoulders)
- creator: @classic_nonchalance (Cléa C.)

- link (Original reel): https://www.instagram.com/reel/Dc_ibD6x6aw/

Episode 9 "Shoulders" of "How to be fluent in suits & tailoring in 48 lessons". Roped: sleeve head sits up and out. Natural: in between. Bald: relaxed and flat. Expression is shape, not construction; Spalla Camicia is a construction and can be roped, natural or bald. Roped does not mean padded; bald does not mean unconstructed. Names vary by tailor (e.g. "con rollino" in Rome). Built from burned-in captions and written caption only; audio not transcribed.
