## Items

### 144. SKWAT (SKAC - Skwat Kameari Art Centre)

Art bookshop and browsing space under the elevated tracks near Kameari Station, Tokyo. Run by art-book distributor twelvebooks, with roughly 60,000 art, photography and design volumes, plus a vinyl record shop (VDS - Vinyl Delivery Service), a cafe and gallery space. Opened November 2024. Address: 3-26-4 Nishikameari, Katsushika-ku, Tokyo 125-0002; 12-minute walk from Kameari Station (JR Joban line); open 11:00-19:00, closed Monday and Tuesday.
