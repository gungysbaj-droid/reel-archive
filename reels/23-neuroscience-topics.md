## Items

### 133. The overview effect

The cognitive shift of awe and changed perspective that astronauts report after seeing Earth from space. The reel's library search returned a conference paper on sustainability and a video among the first hits. NASA's 2025 article describes it as a term coined in 1987 by Frank White in his book of the same name.

- [NASA article](https://www.nasa.gov/centers-and-facilities/johnson/the-overview-effect-astronaut-perspectives-from-25-years-in-low-earth-orbit/)
- [White, The Overview Effect (Internet Archive; reference link, not a rights claim)](https://archive.org/details/overvieweffectsp00whit)

### 134. Alien hand syndrome

A rare condition where a hand or limb acts independently of the person's intent, for example grabbing objects or undoing a completed action, usually after brain injury, surgery or certain conditions (Cleveland Clinic).

- [Cleveland Clinic overview](https://my.clevelandclinic.org/health/diseases/alien-hand-syndrome)
- [Pathophysiology and Treatment of Alien Hand Syndrome (PMC)](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4261226/)

### 135. Charles Bonnet syndrome

Visual hallucinations that can follow major sight loss, often linked to eye conditions like macular degeneration or cataracts. The NHS states it is not caused by a mental health problem or dementia.

- [NHS](https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/charles-bonnet-syndrome/)
- [American Academy of Ophthalmology](https://www.aao.org/eye-health/diseases/what-is-charles-bonnet-syndrome)

### 136. Aphantasia

The absence of voluntary visual mental imagery. Zeman, Dewar and Della Sala described congenital aphantasia in a 2015 letter in Cortex (vol. 73, pp. 378-380).

- [Lives without imagery - congenital aphantasia (Heriot-Watt research portal)](https://researchportal.hw.ac.uk/en/publications/lives-without-imagery-congenital-aphantasia/)

### 137. Hyperthymesia (highly superior autobiographical memory)

Exceptional recall of specific autobiographical events. UC Irvine's CNLM, which first described it in 2006 (participant AJ, later identified as Jill Price), uses the term Highly Superior Autobiographical Memory (HSAM).

- [UCI CNLM: HSAM](https://cnlm.uci.edu/hsam/)
