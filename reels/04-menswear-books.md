# Six books for classic menswear

First three explain how to wear things; last three map the makers. Creator suggests treating them as catalogs, learning one chapter at a time.

## 38. Dressing the Man

Kind: book
Collection: menswear
Creator: Alan Flusser

The foundation: the rules and proportions of classic menswear. The creator calls it his starting point.

Image: ../assets/10-book-1.jpg

- Book details: https://www.harpercollins.com.au/9780060191443/dressing-the-man/

## 39. True Style

Kind: book
Collection: menswear
Creator: G. Bruce Boyer

Commentary on wardrobe pieces, their history and how to wear them. The creator returns to Boyer’s ideas often.

Image: ../assets/11-book-2.jpg

- Book details: https://www.hachettebookgroup.com/titles/g-bruce-boyer/true-style/9780465053995/

## 40. Modern Black Tie: A Guide

Kind: book
Collection: menswear
Creator: Mickael Korausch

An actionable guide to evening wear and building a traditional or alternative black-tie kit, rather than a history-only read.

Image: ../assets/12-book-3.jpg

- Book details: https://www.labowtique.com/product/modern-black-tie/

## 41. Savile Row

Kind: book
Collection: menswear
Creator: James Sherwood

A map of Savile Row’s makers, alongside the history and craft of British tailoring.

Image: ../assets/13-book-4.jpg

- Book details: https://www.thamesandhudson.com/products/savile-row

## 42. The Italian Gentleman

Kind: book
Collection: menswear
Creator: Hugo Jacomet

A map of Italian makers: tailors, cloth merchants, tie makers, shoemakers and other artisans.

Image: ../assets/14-book-5.jpg

- Book details: https://www.thamesandhudson.com/products/the-italian-gentleman

## 43. The Parisian Gentleman

Kind: book
Collection: menswear
Creator: Hugo Jacomet

A map of Parisian houses. The reel names Cifonelli, Camps de Luca, Charvet, Berluti and Aubercy.

Image: ../assets/15-book-6.jpg

- Book details: https://www.thamesandhudson.com/products/the-parisian-gentleman
