# Six books for classic menswear

First three explain how to wear things; last three map the makers. Creator suggests treating them as catalogs, learning one chapter at a time.

## 38. Dressing the Man

Kind: book
Collection: menswear
Creator: Alan Flusser

The foundation: the rules and proportions of classic menswear. The creator calls it his starting point.

Image: ../assets/10-book-1.jpg

- Book details: https://www.harpercollins.com.au/9780060191443/dressing-the-man/

### Contents and coverage

Library-catalog table of contents. Contents and coverage only; no full book text.

Adapting classic clothing to individual physique and complexion; coordinating patterns, choosing silhouettes and collars, and dressing for business casual and formal occasions.

1. Permanent fashion
2. The power of color
3. Proportion: the foundation of style
4. The prerogatives of pattern
5. The suit
6. Odd jackets, trousers, and waistcoats
7. The dress shirt
8. Neckwear
9. Hosiery harmonies
10. Foot decorum
11. Accessories: the major importance of minor things
12. Formalwear: black-and-white etiquette
13. Business casual

Sources (checked 2026-10-11):
- https://catalog.myrichlandlibrary.org/Record/262885
- https://books.google.com/books/about/Dressing_the_Man.html?id=hBnCNOhoh1sC

## 40. Modern Black Tie: A Guide

Kind: book
Collection: menswear
Creator: Mickael Korausch

An actionable guide to evening wear and building a traditional or alternative black-tie kit, rather than a history-only read.

Image: ../assets/12-book-3.jpg

- Book details: https://www.labowtique.com/product/modern-black-tie/

### Contents and coverage

Publisher-confirmed structure; exact chapter names not verified. Contents and coverage only; no full book text.

Ten chapters, a black-tie brand directory and a Stephen Fry foreword. Covers classic and contemporary evening wear, outfit/accessory examples across levels of formality, and the future of the dress code.

## 41. Savile Row

Kind: book
Collection: menswear
Creator: James Sherwood

A map of Savile Row’s makers, alongside the history and craft of British tailoring.

Image: ../assets/13-book-4.jpg

- Book details: https://www.thamesandhudson.com/products/savile-row

### Contents and coverage

Bookseller contents list, with partial library corroboration. Contents and coverage only; no full book text.

Chronological tailoring history through customers, monarchy, military, film and revival; 26 master-tailor profiles plus reference sections on suit anatomy and making.

1. Introduction
2. Royal Savile Row: Monarchs Who Ruled the Row
3. The Beaux on the Row: Men Who Led Fashion
4. Savile Row at War: Tailoring for Heroes
5. Savile Row in Hollywood: Aristocrats of the Silver Screen
6. Savile Row Revival: The New Establishment
7. Savile Row in Fashion: The Row Revival
8. Gentlemen's Requisites
9. Anatomy of a Suit
10. Making a Suit
11. The Language of Savile Row

Caveat: The publisher describes eight chronological themes, while the bookseller list has six numbered sections plus references. The library catalog gives only a partial list. Preserve this source mismatch rather than inventing two missing chapters.

## 42. The Italian Gentleman

Kind: book
Collection: menswear
Creator: Hugo Jacomet

A map of Italian makers: tailors, cloth merchants, tie makers, shoemakers and other artisans.

Image: ../assets/14-book-5.jpg

- Book details: https://www.thamesandhudson.com/products/the-italian-gentleman

### Contents and coverage

2017 ISBN-specific bookseller table of contents. Contents and coverage only; no full book text.

Profiles of more than fifty Italian houses, with mills and cloth merchants, bespoke tailors, sartorial brands, shirtmakers, ties/umbrellas/gloves and shoemakers. Examples include Vitale Barberis Canonico, Zegna, Rubinacci and Kiton.

1. Preface
2. Introduction
3. Mills and Fabric Merchants
4. Bespoke Tailoring
5. Sartorial Brands
6. Shirtmakers
7. Gentlemen's Requisites
8. Shoemakers
9. Address Book
10. Acknowledgments
11. Further Reading
12. Picture Credits
13. Index

Caveat: Use the detailed 2017 ISBN 9780500518571 listing. Slovart lists an inconsistent eight-section outline for the same ISBN, including Pitti Uomo; this is not merged into the six-part structure. The author says the book is a selected personal journey, not a complete catalog or academic history.

## 43. The Parisian Gentleman

Kind: book
Collection: menswear
Creator: Hugo Jacomet

A map of Parisian houses. The reel names Cifonelli, Camps de Luca, Charvet, Berluti and Aubercy.

Image: ../assets/15-book-6.jpg

- Book details: https://www.thamesandhudson.com/products/the-parisian-gentleman

### Contents and coverage

Bookseller table of contents, compact English edition. Contents and coverage only; no full book text.

House-by-house accounts of French tailoring, footwear, shirtmaking, travel goods, perfume and accessories, focused on heritage and craftsmanship.

1. Preface
2. Introduction
3. The Parisian Bespoke Tailoring: Cifonelli; Camps de Luca; Francesco Smalto; Berluti Atelier Arnys
4. Of Men and Shoes: John Lobb; Berluti; Corthay; Aubercy; J.M. Weston; Dimitri Gomez
5. Chemisiers in Paris: Charvet
6. The Art of Travelling: Louis Vuitton special orders; Moynat Paris
7. Parisian Haute Parfumerie: Guerlain; Caron
8. Gentlemen's Requisites: Cartier; St Dupont
9. One of a Kind Houses: Maison Bonnet; Marc Guyot; Simonnot Godard; Mellerio

Caveat: English compact edition ISBN 9780500293966; house names and contents may differ in other editions. Promotional claims such as only bespoke eyewear maker are not independently endorsed.
