# Persona's two-faces shot and its echoes

Film School Dropouts asks "What's the best use of this shot?" about Bergman's Persona (1966) and its two-faces composition: Bibi Andersson and Liv Ullmann overlapped into a single face. The caption (posted 2026-09-20) says the shot has been echoed from La Pointe Courte to Ghost in the Shell, and that David Lynch (Mulholland Drive) and Céline Sciamma (Portrait of a Lady on Fire) cite the film. The reel names no resources, so the archive adds the films with verified pages, plus Criterion's essay on Persona. Film-influence claims are the creator's; links checked 2026-10-11.

## 99. Persona (1966)

Kind: film
Collection: persona-two-faces-shot
Creator: Ingmar Bergman

The film behind the reel's shot. Nurse Alma (Bibi Andersson) and actress Elisabet (Liv Ullmann) in a two-hander shot by Sven Nykvist; the two faces are overlapped, by blocking and compositing, into a single face. Criterion lists the cast and Bergman as director; the composite-face reading is the reel creator's description. Criterion also streams it on the Criterion Channel (subscription).

- Criterion Collection: https://www.criterion.com/films/28491-persona
- Criterion Channel: https://www.criterionchannel.com/persona

## 100. The Persistence of Persona (Criterion essay)

Kind: website
Collection: persona-two-faces-shot
Creator: Criterion Current

Criterion's essay on Persona's lasting hold, opening with the two shots that became its emblems: a boy touching a woman's face on a giant screen, and two women looking at each other in a composite close-up. Best reading to go with the reel. It names Lynch among directors admiring Bergman's work; it does not itself list the specific homages below.

- Essay: https://www.criterion.com/current/posts/3116-the-persistence-of-persona

## 101. La Pointe Courte (1955)

Kind: film
Collection: persona-two-faces-shot
Creator: Agnès Varda

Varda's first feature, a study of a marriage on the rocks in a fishing village. The reel cites it as an example of the one-sided profile composition used for unrequited love. That pairing is the creator's claim; this entry only verifies the film's pages.

- Criterion Collection: https://www.criterion.com/films/524-la-pointe-courte
- Criterion Channel: https://www.criterionchannel.com/la-pointe-courte

## 102. Ghost in the Shell (1995)

Kind: film
Collection: persona-two-faces-shot
Creator: Mamoru Oshii

The reel's example of the shot used for a figuratively and literally shared mind. A fan post compares the recreation frame-by-frame with Persona (user-generated, not an authoritative source); Wikipedia has the film's production background.
